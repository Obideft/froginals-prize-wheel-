THE MISSION

01 — The 50-Badge Collection

The collection contains 50 unique designs across three tiers:

12 Gold Badges

Prestigious gold-tier recruitment insignias.

22 Silver Badges

Tactical silver-tier collection.

16 Wood Badges

Rustic wood-tier recruitment insignias.

Progress is based on different designs in your wallet, not just NFT quantity.

  • 10 NFTs representing 10 different designs = 10/50.
  • 10 copies of the same design = 1/50.
  • All 50 unique designs complete the set, subject to ownership verification.
02 — Connect Your Solana Wallet

Connect the Solana wallet that holds your Recruitment Badge NFTs. The intended Collectible Index will show your wallet, eligible inventory, unique progress, missing designs, completion percentage, and OG eligibility when available.

Ownership should be checked against the official Solana collection and current on-chain holdings. Screenshots, browser storage, or self-reported balances are not proof of ownership. If you transfer a badge out, it may no longer count.

03 — How to Qualify for OG Status

The core requirement is to hold at least one eligible NFT representing every one of the 50 unique badge designs in the same qualifying wallet.

  1. Connect a Solana wallet.
  2. Own eligible NFTs from the official Recruitment Badge collection.
  3. Hold all 50 unique designs; duplicates do not count as extra designs.
  4. Pass official collection and ownership verification.
  5. Meet any published snapshot, deadline, and claim requirements.

50 NFTs alone do not automatically qualify you. If they represent only 35 unique designs, your progress is 35/50.

04 — OG Reward Pack

Completing the collection is intended to qualify collectors for these planned reward categories:

🪂 Future Airdrop Loot Packs

Potential digital collectibles or campaign rewards announced for eligible OG holders.

👕 Merchandise Access

Access opportunities for selected merchandise drops and exclusive designs.

🎟️ Exclusive Access Cards

Digital access for specific community experiences or holder campaigns, where announced.

🥇 Gold Digital Loot Box

The signature completion reward concept, with contents and delivery to be announced.

Reward status: These are planned reward categories, not a promise that every reward is currently live or claimable. Contents, dates, quantities, prices, and distribution rules must be confirmed. Merchandise access may not mean free merchandise.

05 — How to Redeem Rewards
  1. Connect the same wallet that holds the complete set.
  2. Verify ownership of all 50 unique designs.
  3. Confirm collection completion and OG eligibility.
  4. Follow official FROGINALS claim instructions when the claim window opens.
  5. Complete the specific claim or fulfilment steps announced for the reward.
  6. Check claim status and keep the confirmation.

Never share your seed phrase or private key. A legitimate reward claim should never require either.

06 — The 50-Mint Limit & Fair Play

The intended model allows a maximum of 50 Recruitment Badge mints per wallet. This differs from the unique-design requirement: 50 repeated designs do not complete the set.

To make a full set achievable within that limit, the minting system should prevent repeat designs for the same wallet or provide a supported way to obtain missing designs. This must be enforced by minting rules or a trusted backend, not only a website counter.

  • Each wallet has its own collection record.
  • Only verified NFTs from the official collection qualify.
  • Duplicate copies do not increase unique completion.
  • Current ownership may be checked again for reward eligibility.
07 — Official Rules & Reward Status

Before rewards are advertised as claimable, the FROGINALS team should publish the official collection address and badge list, mint limits, duplicate-design rules, ownership verification, snapshots or deadlines, confirmed reward contents, claim window, costs or shipping conditions, and support process.

If an ownership check looks incorrect because of a transfer or indexing delay, use the official support channel to request a recheck.
